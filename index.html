Pharmaceutical Filtration Substrates

Polymer Laboratories Ltd

Church Stretton | 2001–2003

Polymer Laboratories was acquired by Varian in 2005. Agilent Technologies subsequently acquired Varian in 2010. Agilent is therefore a successor organisation, rather than the company that directly employed me.

Developed an improved manufacturing approach for porous polymer filtration substrates, combining control over the material's internal structure with a more precise method of producing consistently sized beads.

MATERIAL DEVELOPMENT

Creating an open porous structure

A polystyrene mixture was combined with a temporary filler. Subsequent removal of the filler by evaporation left an open structure within the polymer.

01
Polystyrene and removable filler

The filler occupied space within the polymer mixture. Removing it by evaporation left the open porous structure required in the filtration substrate.

MANUFACTURING PROCESS

Controlled droplet formation

Developed a process using an outer tube and laminar flow to form droplets of consistent size, which could then be carried through the flow for curing.

02
Porous-tube bead formation

The polymer mixture was forced through a porous inner tube. Material emerging through the pores formed beads that grew to a controlled size before entering the laminar flow within the surrounding outer tube.

The flowing medium then carried the formed beads onwards for curing.

02
Laminar-flow droplet formation

The controlled flow allowed droplets to form at a consistent size, rather than producing a bulk mixture of polymer beads with a wide range of dimensions.

PROCESS IMPROVEMENT

Consistent bead sizing through formation

The formed droplets could be cured to produce beads of a much more controlled size.

PREVIOUS METHOD
Bulk production and sieving

Produce batches of beads, blend them and sieve to separate the required approximate size range.

→
IMPROVED APPROACH
Controlled droplet formation

Form droplets at a consistent size and cure them, producing more uniform beads directly through the process.

Engineering and process development

Developed a manufacturing approach that addressed both the internal porosity of the polymer substrate and the consistency of the finished bead size.

The work moved away from relying solely on post-production blending and sieving, towards controlling particle size during formation.

Conceptual illustrations of the material structure and manufacturing process, not reconstructions of the original production equipment.
